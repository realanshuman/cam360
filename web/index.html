questions

is Cam360 really free?

yes. every feature, with no trial, no paid tier and no account. there are no servers to pay for, because everything runs on your own computer.

does my video ever leave my computer?

no. Cam360 makes no network requests at all. your video is changed inside the browser tab and handed straight to the call, and even the ai model ships inside the extension.

does it work in the zoom or discord desktop app?

no, only on websites in your browser. no browser extension can reach a desktop app. use the web version of zoom or discord, or pair Cam360 with obs virtual camera.

why doesn't the ai background work on google meet?

meet blocks the webassembly the ai model needs. switch the cut out method to green screen and backgrounds work there too. every other effect works on meet as normal.

which browsers does it work in?

chrome, and the browsers built on it: edge, brave and arc. firefox and safari aren't supported today.
