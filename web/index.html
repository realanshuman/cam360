Features

Everything the call app forgot to give you.

Websites decide what camera controls you get. Cam360 puts them back, in one place, on every site.

Frame it right

Mirror, flip, and rotate in 90 degree steps. Digital zoom up to 250 percent when you want a tighter crop.

Fix the lighting

Brightness, contrast, saturation, and hue, plus a low light boost for dim rooms. Six presets when one click is enough.

Replace the background

Blur it, fill it with colour, drop in a scene or your own photo, or run an animated backdrop behind you.

Two ways to cut out

AI segmentation for quality with no green screen, or colour keying that keeps working on sites which block the model.

Step away cleanly

Freeze the frame or show a be right back card without dropping your video. Save the processed frame as a PNG.

Add your details

A name tag, a logo watermark, and a live clock, rendered straight into the outgoing feed.

Backgrounds

Two ways to cut yourself out.

Choose what goes behind you, then choose how you get separated from it. The second choice is what makes backgrounds work everywhere.

  • AI segmentation. A selfie segmentation model runs on your device for the cleanest edge, with no green screen needed.
  • Green screen. Colour keying in plain JavaScript, so background replacement still works on strict sites like Google Meet.
  • Bundled, not streamed. The model ships inside the extension, so it loads with your network unplugged.

In the call

Adjust without leaving the meeting.

Nobody wants to open a settings page while twelve people watch. Press Alt, Shift, and C to drop a control panel on top of the call, then drag it out of your way.

  • Every change lands on the live stream instantly.
  • The panel and the toolbar popup stay in sync.
  • Switch Cam360 off and your raw camera returns at once.

How it works

It intercepts the camera, not the app.

Cam360 does not need a site to support it. It answers the camera request itself, which is why one setup covers every video site you use.

1

A site asks for your camera

Cam360 hooks the request before the page has a chance to run its own code.

2

Your feed runs through a canvas

Each frame is processed on your device in real time, using the GPU where the browser allows it.

3

The site receives the result

Meet, Discord, and the rest get the finished stream and treat it as an ordinary camera.

Privacy

Your video never leaves the machine.

This is the part worth being specific about, because video is not the kind of data you hand over casually.

  • No network requests. Cam360 does not call a server, because it does not have one.
  • No account, no sign in, no analytics, no crash reporting.
  • Settings stay in local browser storage. Uploaded backgrounds never move off disk.
  • The source is public, so none of the above has to be taken on faith.

Processing happens in the page

The camera stream is captured, drawn to a canvas, and handed back inside the same tab. Nothing is uploaded at any point, including the AI background, whose model is bundled with the extension rather than fetched from a CDN.

Install

Four steps, about a minute.

Cam360 is not on the Chrome Web Store yet, so it loads as an unpacked extension. That also means you can read every line before you run it.

Get the files

Download the repository as a ZIP and unpack it, or run git clone https://github.com/realanshuman/cam360.git

Open the extensions page

Go to chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode in the top right corner.

Load it unpacked

Choose Load unpacked, then select the project folder, the one containing manifest.json

Pin it and start a call

Pin Cam360 to the toolbar, open any site that uses your camera, and adjust from the popup or press Alt, Shift, C.

FAQ

Questions worth answering honestly.

Does it work in the Discord or Zoom desktop app?

No. A Chrome extension can only reach video inside the browser, so native desktop apps are out of its range. Reaching those needs a system level virtual camera, which is a separate driver rather than an extension. Use the web version of those apps, or pair Cam360 with OBS Virtual Camera when you need the desktop client.

Does my video ever leave my computer?

No. Cam360 makes no network requests and includes no analytics. Frames are captured, processed, and handed back to the site inside the same browser tab, and the AI model ships inside the extension instead of being downloaded at runtime.

Why is the AI background unavailable on Google Meet?

Meet sets a strict content security policy that stops extensions from loading WebAssembly into its page, which is what the segmentation model needs. Switch the cut out method to green screen and background replacement works there too. Every other effect, including lighting, framing, and overlays, keeps working normally.

Which browsers are supported?

Chrome and other Chromium browsers that support Manifest V3, including Microsoft Edge, Brave, and Arc. Firefox and Safari use a different extension model and are not supported today.

Will it slow down my machine?

Effects run on the GPU where the browser allows it, and the AI mask is computed at a reduced resolution rather than full frame. The heaviest setting is green screen keying at 1080p, since that inspects pixels directly. Turning Cam360 off returns your raw camera immediately, with no processing at all.

Can I use my own image or video as a background?

Yes. Upload any image for a still background, or any video file for a moving one, and it is composited behind you in real time. There are also five built in gradient scenes and two animated backdrops drawn by the extension itself.
