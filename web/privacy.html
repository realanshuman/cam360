Privacy policy

Effective 27 September 2026. This is short because there is very little to disclose.

What Cam360 collects

Nothing. Cam360 has no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting, no accounts, and no servers. The extension makes no network requests of any kind.

Your camera

Cam360 processes your camera feed frame by frame on your own device, inside your browser, and hands the result to the website you are using. Video is never recorded, stored, or transmitted by the extension. The AI background model is bundled inside the extension and runs locally, so frames are not sent anywhere for processing.

What is stored, and where

Your settings, such as slider values, chosen backgrounds, and any images you upload for backgrounds or overlays, are kept in Chrome's local extension storage on your computer. They never leave your machine. Snapshots you save go straight to your own downloads folder. Uninstalling the extension removes its stored settings.

Third parties

No data is shared with anyone, because no data is collected. Cam360 contains no third party trackers or SDKs.

Changes and contact

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions are welcome by email at hi@realanshuman.com, or as issues on the public support tracker.
